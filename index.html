The short version

            

            

                
                

                    
I write Kotlin for a living and for fun. Most of my time goes into Android: Compose UIs, the plumbing underneath them, and the security questions nobody likes to think about — is this device rooted, can this request be trusted?

                    
I care about small things: a spring that settles just right, an icon that lines up on the pixel grid, an APK you can verify yourself. Open source is where most of that work lives.
